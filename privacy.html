Bravia Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Bravia Launcher is a personal Android TV launcher built by Mohsen Parchami for use on a single personal TV. It is not a commercial product.

What it accesses

The app reads and edits events in one shared Google Calendar, so the calendar can be viewed and updated from the TV. It does not access any other calendars or any other Google data.

Where data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the TV the app runs on. Calendar events are fetched from Google when needed and are not copied to any other server.

Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone. There are no ads, analytics or third-party services beyond Google Calendar itself.
