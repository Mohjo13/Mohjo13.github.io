Technical Systems

Systems I designed and built, shown in motion. Pick a project, then a system.

Camera System

A dynamic camera that shifts angle to build tension and point the player at threats. Smooth transitions keep the flow cinematic, and each level tunes the behaviour to its own layout and pacing.

Unity, C#

Movement Mechanics

Fast, responsive traversal with a directional dash for repositioning, clearing gaps and keeping momentum under pressure. Speed, timing and cooldown are all exposed for tuning.

Unity, C#

Shaders

Cooldown feedback drawn by shaders instead of UI, reflective and refractive materials that distort what sits behind them, and ripples that react as the player moves through liquid.

Unity, Shaders

Lights and Post Production

Lighting that carries the mood through controlled light, shadow and colour. Grading, exposure and bloom add depth and atmosphere.

Unity, Post-processing

Combat System

Data-driven melee with light and heavy attacks, combos and dodge. Every attack is a ScriptableObject, so timing windows, hitbox phases and animation are tuned per asset in the Inspector. Input buffering keeps combos responsive mid-animation.

Unity 6, C#, ScriptableObjects

Parry System

A timing-based parry with spark feedback that knocks the enemy into a stagger state. The parry window lives in data, so designers tune startup and recovery per weapon.

C#, Enemy AI states, Particles

Custom Editor Tool

A custom Unity window for authoring weapons and attacks. Drag phase markers on a timeline, preview hitbox gizmos live in the scene and tune combo sequences in one window.

Unity EditorWindow, Gizmos

Streak HUD and Shaders

A combo streak HUD where attack icons fly into a row and combo names dissolve out, using a dissolve shader written in HLSL. The panel glow is hand-written too, since HDRP blocks Shader Graph on Canvas.

HLSL, TextMesh Pro SDF
